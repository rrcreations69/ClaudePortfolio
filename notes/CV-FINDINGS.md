## ⚠️ Tone — the CV's language must not be copied across

Standard CV register that should **not** survive into the site:

> "Results-driven", "Adept at translating…", "strong track record", "driving full system lifecycle
> delivery"

The design direction is plain and evidence-led. Also note these CV claims are unquantified —
"improving workflow efficiency and reducing manual overhead", "improving system reliability and user
experience". They are acceptable as qualitative statements and **must not be given numbers** on the
site to make them look stronger.
